Barbados Budget – What Citizens Pay & What They Get

Citizen view of government revenue and spending • FY2025/26 revised estimates • BDS$ millions
Ribbon thickness is proportional to the amount of money. Incoming money is shown in grey; the fiscal result turns green for a surplus and red for a deficit.

How to read it

Left side: where government money comes from.

Right side: a citizen-friendly grouping of what that money pays for.

Middle: the full spending envelope for the year. Every ribbon is drawn to scale, so a ribbon twice as thick carries twice the money.

About the citizen view

The right-hand categories are the Barbados government's own functional classification of spending (Table 5 of the Estimates), relabelled in plain language. They are not an editorial invention.

Each category names the official function it comes from — hover any flow to see it. Two categories combine functions: housing with environmental protection, and police and courts with defence.

Fiscal result

If revenue is less than spending, the chart shows a red deficit / borrowing need entering on the left.

If revenue is greater than spending, it shows a green surplus leaving on the right.

Where these figures come from

      A source is marked verified only where the build reconciles every figure it provides against the totals printed in that document. Anything still marked unverified has not been checked line by line.

      Where the numbers come from: the files in data/ are generated by tools/build_data.py straight from the official Estimates PDFs in sources/, and the build refuses to emit a file unless every figure reconciles to the totals printed in those documents. To change a year's figures, rebuild rather than editing the JSON by hand. If data/ cannot be loaded (for example when opening this file directly from disk in some browsers), the chart falls back to the copy embedded under EDIT DATA HERE in this file.
